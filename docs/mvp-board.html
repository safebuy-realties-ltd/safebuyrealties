ADR-0001
Architecture Decision Record. A decision important enough to be written down as a numbered document and kept. An open one is a question waiting for an answer, not a task waiting for a developer.
D1 to D5
The short name each of those five decisions carried before it was written up. D2 and ADR-0002 are the same decision under two names.
EXT-1
External input. Something supplied from outside this repository: an account, a domain, a bucket, a published policy, a signature, a booked vendor.

Go-live gate. A condition that has to hold before the product goes in front of real users and real money.
E4-S2
Story 2 of epic 4. Every buildable row in the table above is named this way, so E2-S1 is the first story of the money epic.
S, M, L
The published size of a story in developer-days. S is about a day, M is two to four, L is about a week. Every day figure on this page is the upper bound of that range.
CH-1, DOCS-4
A chore and a documentation story. Neither is user-facing, and both are written, reviewed and merged like any other row.
QA-015
A numbered finding from the quality audit that ran before the handover week.
W1 to W6
The waves that follow the handover week. D1 to D5 in the Day column mean the week's days; W1 onwards mean the waves after it. All six are closed. A wave opens when something arrives from outside the repository, not on a calendar: W4 opened because a stakeholder answered ADR-0001, W5 because an approved specification turned up, W6 because a deployment target was chosen, which is the first one opened by a decision rather than by a document.

A pull request number in this repository. A number on this page always means a pull request, never a story.

Words borrowed from finance, law and email

DD
Due diligence. The checks run on a property and on the person selling it before any money moves. This is the part of the product that exists today.
KYC
Know your customer. The identity check on a user before that user is allowed to move money.
PoA
Power of Attorney. The instrument a buyer signs so the platform can act on their behalf. A lawyer drafts it, not a developer.
NDPR
Nigeria Data Protection Regulation, and the Act that now carries it. It sets the privacy notice and the retention duties EXT-5 covers.
CBN
Central Bank of Nigeria. Its rules are what turn holding somebody else's money into a regulated activity rather than a feature.
AML
Anti-money-laundering. The reporting and record-keeping duties that arrive with holding client funds.
SPF, DKIM, DMARC
Three records published in a domain's DNS settings. Together they let a receiving mail server believe a message really came from that domain. Without them, transactional email is dropped or filed as spam.
SMTP
The protocol, and the credentials a mail provider issues, that let an application send email at all.
Escrow
Holding a buyer's money between payment and completion instead of passing it straight to the seller. This platform does that, settled by ADR-0002 on 2026-08-05, so the money is a liability owed to the parties rather than income and the account it sits in is held apart from operating cash. That is what makes E2-S1 a regulated flow rather than a payout form.
